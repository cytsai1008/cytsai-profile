# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed professional and personal audience: engineering peers and potential employers arriving via GitHub or blog posts, alongside colleagues and acquaintances who want to know the person behind the work. They land here to read, to check who CYTsai is, or to get in touch — often from a link, rarely with a task deadline.

## Product Purpose

The professional/real-life counterpart to cytsai-profile: a personal site under the CYTsai identity (cytsai1008 for anything GitHub-related) collecting writing, career context, and ways to connect. Success is a visitor leaving with an accurate impression of a capable developer — and finding the blog worth returning to.

## Positioning

A developer who ships and writes. Every page should reinforce both halves: working code exists (GitHub), and the thinking behind it is written down (blog).

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: no single funnel — the home page routes with equal weight to reading the blog, viewing career/about info, and connecting (GitHub, email, LinkedIn). All three must be one interaction from the fold.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "a developer who ships and writes."
- Belief ladder: (1) this person is a real, active developer → (2) the writing is worth reading → (3) worth contacting/following.
- Proof on hand: the blog itself and the GitHub profile (cytsai1008). No testimonials or press; don't fabricate any.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy — the current cytsai-profile voice, sharpened: less cute, more matter-of-fact. Quiet competence; the engineer you'd want reviewing your PR. Programmer texture (mono accents, sharp corners, true-dark mode, blue accent) as seasoning, never costume.

## Anti-references

- Corporate LinkedIn clone: stiff, buzzwordy, headshot-and-blazer energy.
- Terminal cosplay: fake CLI prompts, blinking cursors, matrix-green. Mono accents are fine; pretending to be a shell is not.

## Design Principles

- Ships and writes: show real output (posts, repos), never placeholder bravado.
- Sharpened calm: keep the accessible, unhurried base; cut the cuteness.
- Seasoning, not costume: programmer texture stays subordinate to legibility.
- Equal doors: blog, about/career, and contact are peers — no forced funnel.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA maintained from the parent codebase: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected throughout, Atkinson Hyperlegible for latin text, semantic HTML, keyboard-reachable interactive islands.
